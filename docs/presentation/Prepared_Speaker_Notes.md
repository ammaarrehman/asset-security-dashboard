## Slide 1

"Hi, I'm Ammaar, and this is my Cyber Ready Clinic capstone.

I built a dashboard that helps small organizations keep track of their devices and basic security information.

All the devices in this demo are fictional, so I can show how it works without using real client data."

## Slide 2

"During Discover 5 in Cyber Ready Clinic, I learned how important it is to know what devices an organization has and how they're protected.

If that information is missing or out of date, it's harder to tell what needs attention.

We also talked about how you can't really check endpoint protection coverage if you don't know which devices you have. That idea is what inspired this project."

Pause briefly and point to the missing information in the table.

## Slide 3

"My dashboard puts device information in one place.

You can record the status of updates, security software, and backups.

Based on those records, it highlights gaps and suggests what to check next.

I also made missing information visible as Unknown, because not knowing the status of a device is something a team still needs to check."

Point across the diagram.

## Slide 4

"This laptop's record shows overdue updates, so the dashboard flags it.

Once someone confirms the updates are installed, they update the record and the recorded finding clears."

## Slide 6

"CRC taught me why it matters to know what devices you have.

Building this also taught me to keep missing information visible.

The dashboard organizes what people record. It doesn't scan devices or verify their security.

The bigger lesson for me is that a tool alone doesn't solve the problem. Someone still has to verify the information and act on it.

If I kept developing this, I'd want to connect it to real device management tools.

Thank you."
