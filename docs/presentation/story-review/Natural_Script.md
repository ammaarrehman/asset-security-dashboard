## Slide 2: Why I decided to build this (0:20 to 0:55)

> During Discover 5 in Cyber Ready Clinic, I learned how important it is to know what devices an organization has and how they’re protected. If that information is missing or out of date, it’s harder to tell what needs attention.

Point to the blanks, the question mark, and “Not sure.” Give the audience a moment to notice the missing answers. Discover 5 is the personal learning connection. Do not describe client records or imply deployment.

## Slide 3: What my dashboard does (0:55 to 1:20)

> My dashboard puts device information in one place. You can record the status of updates, security software, and backups. Based on those records, it highlights gaps and suggests what to check next.

Point across the existing diagram as you explain the flow. “Security software” explains the word “protection” in the diagram.

## Slide 4: Here’s an example (1:20 to 1:40)
