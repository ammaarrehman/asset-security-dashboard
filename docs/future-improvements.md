# Future improvement ideas

These ideas predate the published redesign. The status notes distinguish delivered features from optional future refinements; this is not a committed roadmap.

- **Delivered: actionable priority findings on the overview.** The published dashboard shows priority follow-ups with affected devices and links to details, using explicit, documented rules and recorded evidence.
- **Better chart readability and data density:** Make labels, counts, and scales easier to read, and use available space more effectively, especially on laptop screens. Avoid invented history or risk scores.
- **Improved secondary typography:** Review the size, contrast, and hierarchy of metadata, chart labels, and supporting text across desktop and mobile layouts.
- **Delivered: device and remediation context.** The published asset details and findings combine recorded ownership, control status, review dates, unknown values, and recommended next steps.

Any future change should preserve the browser-only educational scope, fictional demo data, deterministic hygiene rules, explicit unknown values, shared validation, atomic CSV imports, and formula protection on export. These documentation updates do not change application functionality or visual design.
